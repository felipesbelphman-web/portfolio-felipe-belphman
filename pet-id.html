The project

Pet ID explores a simple digital profile where important information about a pet can be presented clearly and accessed from different devices.

The experience is designed around quick identification, clear contact information, and an interface that remains easy to understand during a stressful situation.

The challenge

When a pet is lost, information needs to be communicated quickly. A complicated interface or unclear contact details can delay communication with the guardian.

The challenge was to organize essential information in a friendly, accessible, and mobile-first experience.

The solution

The interface prioritizes the pet’s identity, guardian contact options, and important details using clear hierarchy and responsive components.
