My creative journey began with graphic design in 2019. I learned Photoshop, Illustrator and branding — and discovered Figma.

In 2023, I started turning my designs into code. I started with HTML, CSS and JavaScript. After moving to Dublin, I committed to becoming a front-end developer.

Today, I combine design thinking with development. I build responsive, accessible products with JavaScript, TypeScript, React and Next.js — and I’m ready to contribute, learn and grow.
