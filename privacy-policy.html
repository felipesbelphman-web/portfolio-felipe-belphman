Privacy Policy

Last updated: 15 September 2026

1. Introduction

This Privacy Policy explains how Felipe S Bephman collects, uses, and protects personal information when you visit this portfolio or contact me about my services.

This portfolio is operated from Ireland and is intended to present my work and allow potential clients, employers, and collaborators to contact me.

2. Information I Collect

2.1 Information you provide by email

When you use the contact form, you may provide:

  • Your name
  • Your email address
  • The type of enquiry
  • Your message
  • Any other information you choose to include

The contact form creates an email using your device’s email application. This website does not store the form submission in its own database.

2.2 Information shared through WhatsApp

If you choose to contact me through WhatsApp, your phone number, profile information, message, and any files you send will be processed through WhatsApp. WhatsApp’s own privacy terms also apply to that communication.

2.3 Technical information

The website does not currently use analytics or advertising trackers. The website hosting provider may process limited technical information, such as IP address, browser information, and server logs, to deliver and secure the website.

3. Cookies and Analytics

This portfolio does not currently set analytics, advertising, or marketing cookies.

External services opened through links, including WhatsApp and social media platforms, may use their own cookies according to their privacy policies.

4. How I Use Your Information

I use the information you provide to:

  • Respond to enquiries
  • Discuss projects or employment opportunities
  • Provide information you request
  • Maintain records of relevant communications
  • Protect the website and prevent misuse
  • Comply with applicable legal obligations

5. Legal Bases for Processing

Where the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) applies, personal information may be processed because:

  • You have given consent
  • Processing is necessary to respond to your request or take steps before entering a contract
  • Processing is necessary for legitimate interests, such as communicating with potential clients or protecting the website
  • Processing is necessary to comply with a legal obligation

6. How Information Is Shared

I do not sell or rent your personal information. Information may be processed by services needed to operate communications and the website, including:

  • Email service providers
  • WhatsApp, when you choose to use it
  • The website hosting provider
  • Professional advisers or public authorities when required by law

7. International Data Transfers

Some external service providers may process information outside Ireland or the European Economic Area. Where required, those providers are expected to use recognised safeguards for international data transfers.

8. Data Retention

Personal information is retained only for as long as reasonably necessary to respond to your enquiry, maintain relevant business records, resolve disputes, or comply with legal obligations.

You may request deletion of your personal information, subject to any information that must be retained by law.

9. Data Security

Reasonable technical and organisational measures are used to protect personal information. However, no internet transmission or electronic storage method can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

10. Your Data Protection Rights

Depending on applicable law, including the GDPR, you may have the right to:

  • Request access to your personal information
  • Request correction of inaccurate information
  • Request deletion of your information
  • Restrict certain processing
  • Object to certain processing
  • Request data portability where applicable
  • Withdraw consent at any time

You may also lodge a complaint with the Irish Data Protection Commission or another competent supervisory authority.

11. Children’s Privacy

This portfolio and its services are not directed to children. I do not knowingly collect personal information from children through this website.

12. Third-Party Links

This website may contain links to external websites and services. I am not responsible for the privacy practices or content of those third parties.

13. Changes to This Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated when the website, its services, or legal requirements change. The date at the top of this page will show the latest revision.

For privacy questions or requests, contact:
