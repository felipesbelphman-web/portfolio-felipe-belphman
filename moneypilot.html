04 / PRODUCT THINKING

Decisions behind the interface.

DESIGN DECISION

Decision-first hierarchy

Instead of presenting every financial metric at once, the dashboard prioritizes the information that helps users decide what to do next: current balance, cash flow, unusual spending, goal progress, and recommended actions.

ACCESSIBILITY

Clarity across themes and devices

Day and Night modes preserve hierarchy and contrast. Responsive layouts, visible focus states, understandable labels, reduced visual noise, and consistent interaction patterns support different devices and user needs.

CORE CHALLENGE

Complex data without complexity

The main challenge was combining transactions, budgets, goals, investments, and AI guidance without creating an interface that feels like professional accounting software. Progressive disclosure keeps advanced information available without overwhelming the user.

KEY LEARNING

Trust must be designed and engineered

Financial products require more than polished screens. Clear language, predictable feedback, user-owned data, RLS policies, validation, error handling, and meaningful tests all contribute to the same product quality: trust.
